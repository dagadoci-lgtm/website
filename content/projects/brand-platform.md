## The challenge

The team had outgrown a collection of one-off landing pages. Every launch introduced a new visual pattern, and routine updates required engineering support.

## The approach

I mapped the recurring content needs, reduced them to a small set of composable patterns, and built a shared system that connected design decisions directly to production components.

The work focused on three principles:

- Make the common path fast for editors.
- Keep visual choices intentional and constrained.
- Let the system grow without creating another redesign project.

## The outcome

The resulting platform gave the team a coherent visual language, a faster publishing workflow, and a foundation that could support new campaigns without duplicating implementation work.
