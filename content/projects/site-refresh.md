## The challenge

A valuable archive had become difficult to navigate and costly to maintain. Readers struggled to discover related material, while editors worked around rigid templates.

## The approach

I paired a lightweight content audit with a new information hierarchy, reusable editorial layouts, and a typography system designed for long-form reading.

Accessibility and page weight were treated as product requirements throughout the build rather than checks at the end.

## The outcome

The refreshed site created clearer paths through the archive, improved the reading experience on small screens, and gave editors more flexibility without sacrificing consistency.
